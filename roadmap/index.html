Roadmap¶

DEIStools exists to collect the published methods for estimating impedance from multi-frequency signals of slowly time-varying systems, so that they can be compared and each one's limitations studied. That makes the list of methods not yet implemented part of the specification rather than a backlog.

Done¶

item outcome
2 BLA-style short-time Fourier method per-burst BLA: noise level calibrated to 0.3% against the observed scatter, odd/even distortion with no false alarm on a linear cell, and a frequency-correlation test that separates noise from time variation (9–19 against 0.73). The varying-cell case is item 2b.
1 Lock-in amplifier and Goertzel filter both implemented, with examples 07 and 08 and 15 tests. Both turned out to be the STFT by another route — Goertzel to 1e-10, the lock-in to 9e-14 — which makes them independent checks on it rather than fourth and fifth methods
4 Variance estimation for DMFA extract_impedance_with_error, following Chukwu 2022 eq. (19)–(20). Predicted/observed = 0.997 across three noise configurations. The comparison against traditional band filtering is still open.
6 Other detrending strategies moving average, Butterworth low-pass removal and polynomial fit, measured against differencing, with example 09. Conclusion: only differencing can be a BLTVA weighting factor, and only the polynomial fit helps DMFA — and differencing buys columns rather than accuracy. See detrending

Planned¶

item why
2b The three-step estimator of Pintelon 2013 §V-B the per-burst BLA reports noise and distortion on a cell that is steady inside a burst, but on a varying one the time-variation skirt swamps both. Modelling the variation first — fitting the \(H_p\) branches at the non-harmonic bins — would lift that. Its step 3 cannot be transcribed as published -- the central differences it uses need equispaced harmonics, and on this grid they cost 156x on the first derivative while the second-difference formula diverges under refinement. The implementation note measures that and gives the replacement, so this is specified rather than blocked
3 End-to-end check of DMFA against an independent implementation the definition, filter and variance are now verified against Koster 2017, Battistel 2016/2019 and Chukwu 2022, and three bugs were fixed; what is left is a comparison against code that is not this code — see the notes
4b Slepian-basis DMFA the Fourier basis forces a periodicity that band-limitedness does not require; a DPSS basis is 5× better at the leading edge for the same cost
5 BLTVA with the Gaussian method for automatic \(N_q\) and \(N_p\) selection would remove the two parameters that currently need judgement — and the \(N_q\) result makes this more valuable than it looked, since \(N_q\) matters more than the detrending choice
6b Joint trend + envelope solve for DMFA a filter cannot separate a trend from the signal when both are in the same bins; putting the trend in the design matrix returns every affected tone to its trend-free accuracy. The normal equations are block-arrow and can be formed by FFT
6c Trend-aware demodulation for the lock-in method='ls' already carries a local slope and is 11× better than mixing on a drifting cell. Extending it to a local quadratic, and to STFT's windows, is cheap and untested

Also open: uncertainty estimation for STFT and DMFA, deferred until the literature on it has been worked through. BLTVA has it because Hallemans et al. derive it; offering error bars for the other two without that grounding would be worse than offering none.

Known gaps¶

  • Loading measured data. deistools.processing.data_loader opens a Tkinter file dialog and takes no path argument, so it cannot be called from a script, a test or a headless session. cut_signal_extemes in data_preparation is an unimplemented stub, and its name is misspelled.
  • Record trimming is left to the user. The examples all generate whole numbers of periods. bltva refuses a record that is not one; STFT and DMFA do not check.
  • No diffusion model. All four simulated systems are RC-like. There is no Warburg or finite-length diffusion element, which is what most electrochemists would want to validate against.

How a new estimator should arrive¶

To keep comparisons honest, a new method should come with:

  1. a first-run example — one estimation at good settings, showing the call and what comes back;
  2. a parameter example — each parameter swept against a known ground truth;
  3. an all-systems example — the method on all four simulated systems;
  4. entries in the four comparison scripts;
  5. a row in the status table on the home page, stating honestly what it has been validated against.

The existing examples 04–06, 10–15 and 20–23 follow exactly this pattern.
